Added the `assume_role_arn` setting to the Amazon Kinesis connector, with the optional `assume_role_external_id` and `sts_endpoint` settings. When it is set, the connector assumes the IAM role through AWS STS, and writes to Kinesis with the temporary credentials of the role. The connector gets new temporary credentials before they expire. With a role in another AWS account, the Kinesis action can write to a stream in that account.
