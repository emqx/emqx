Added a new plugin `emqx_bcast` that provides product-level device messaging APIs: `PubBroadcast` (broadcast to all online devices of a product), `BatchPub` (batch delivery to a device list with offline storage and QoS 1 acknowledgement tracking), and `RegisterMessage` (message pre-registration with content deduplication and TTL refresh).

The plugin exposes a unified endpoint `POST /api/v5/plugin_api/emqx_bcast/pub`, delivers messages directly to connected client channels, replays pending QoS 1 deliveries on device reconnect, and exports Prometheus metrics at `GET /api/v5/plugin_api/emqx_bcast/metrics`.
