Fixed NATS Gateway subject translation so NATS subjects cannot acquire MQTT wildcard or shared-subscription semantics, and MQTT topics with ambiguous token boundaries are not delivered to NATS subscribers.

Subjects containing `/`, `+`, or `#`, or starting with `$share.`, `$queue.`, or `$exclusive.`, are rejected. These restrictions also apply to reply subjects in `PUB` and `HPUB` requests; reply subjects must not contain NATS wildcards.

NATS `>` subscriptions and JWT permissions now require at least one trailing subject token. Unsupported subjects in NATS User JWT permissions are rejected during connection authentication.

Queue-group names containing MQTT separators or wildcards are rejected, and the resulting subscription filter must remain consistent with the requested NATS subject.

JWT publish and subscribe permissions are matched directly using NATS subject semantics. Wildcard deny rules now cover subjects starting with `$`, preventing MQTT wildcard exclusions from bypassing JWT restrictions.

Publish JWT permissions are checked against the original frame subject before message ingress. EMQX ACL authorization applies to the resulting MQTT topic after ingress rewrites.
