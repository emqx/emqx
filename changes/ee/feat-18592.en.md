Added `license.tps_alarm_sustain_duration`, which holds back the license message-throughput alarm until the limit has been exceeded continuously for that long.

Workloads with brief TPS bursts used to raise the alarm on a single over-limit sample, even when the steady-state throughput stayed well below the licensed limit. Setting a duration, for example `5m`, alarms only on sustained overload; a sample at or below the limit resets the counter. The default is 1 minute, and the accepted range is `0s` to `10m`.
