Added `license.tps_alarm_trigger_duration`, which holds back the license message-throughput alarm until the limit has been exceeded continuously for that long.

Workloads with brief traffic spikes raised the alarm on a single over-limit sample, even when the steady-state throughput stayed well below the licensed limit. Setting a duration, for example `1h`, alarms only on sustained overload; a sample at or below the limit restarts the count. The default `0s` keeps the previous behaviour of alarming on the first sample above the limit. The check runs on the license resource sampling interval, currently 5s, so the duration takes effect in units of that interval.
