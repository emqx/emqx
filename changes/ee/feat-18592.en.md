Added `license.tps_alarm_sustain_duration`, which holds back the license message-throughput alarm until the limit has been exceeded continuously for that long.

Workloads with brief traffic spikes raised the alarm on a single over-limit sample, even when the steady-state throughput stayed well below the licensed limit. Setting a duration, for example `1h`, alarms only on sustained overload; a sample at or below the limit restarts the count. The default value is 30 minutes.
