Fixed an issue where the Snowflake connector's configured TLS (`ssl`) settings were not applied to its outbound HTTPS connections (both Streaming and Aggregated modes). Settings such as `verify`, `cacertfile`, client certificates, and `server_name_indication` were accepted and displayed but had no effect on the actual connections. The configured values are now honoured. Connectors that never customized the `ssl` settings keep the previous connection behavior.
