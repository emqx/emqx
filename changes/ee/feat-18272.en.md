MQTT WebSocket listeners gained a new `proxy_address_allow` option: a list of IPv4 or IPv6 CIDR ranges specifying which sources (typically the load balancers or reverse proxies in front of EMQX) are trusted to carry the original client address and port in the `proxy_address_header` and `proxy_port_header` headers. The listener derives the client address from the forwarded header only for connections coming from trusted sources; otherwise the socket source address is used. The default (`["0.0.0.0/0", "::/0"]`) trusts all sources.

When the header lists several addresses, entries are now scanned from right to left, skipping trusted intermediate proxies; the first entry outside the trusted ranges is used as the client address. When all entries are trusted (as under the default), the leftmost entry is used, matching the previous extraction behavior.
