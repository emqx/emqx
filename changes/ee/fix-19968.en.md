Fixed QoS 1 BatchPub recovery and accounting in the `emqx_bcast` plugin.
`PendingCount` now decreases after a partial acknowledgement. An unacknowledged
delivery is retried after a clean-session reconnect. During a rolling upgrade,
the plugin keeps ACK accounting consistent and recovers pending deliveries after
an index owner handover.

Plugin-generated 429 responses now write throttled warnings with request and
quota details. Invalid plugin configuration values are rejected at write time.
