Fixed MQTT-SN session wake-up authorization for DTLS clients authenticated with verified client certificates. A certificate-bound session can be resumed from a new association only when the incoming certificate subject DN and CN match. Wake-up without a client certificate or with different subject values is rejected, while a reissued certificate with the same DN and CN remains accepted.

Plaintext UDP and DTLS clients without a certificate retain the legacy ClientId-only PINGREQ wake-up behavior.
