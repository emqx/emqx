Fixed MQTT-SN session wake-up authorization for DTLS listeners configured with client certificate verification. Plaintext UDP and DTLS connections without a client certificate retain the legacy ClientId-only PINGREQ wake-up behavior. When a DTLS client certificate is present, a new association may wake a session only when its certificate subject DN and CN match the values recorded with that session; a missing client certificate or different subject DN/CN is rejected. A different certificate with the same subject DN and CN is accepted. CleanSession=true may still enter asleep, but a new clean CONNECT starts a new session instead of resuming the old queued messages or subscriptions.
