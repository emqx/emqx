The publish rate limiter now exposes a dedicated metric for rejected QoS 1 and QoS 2 publishes.

Previously, only QoS 0 publishes stopped by the publish rate limiter were counted, as
`messages.dropped.quota_exceeded` (Prometheus: `emqx_messages_dropped_quota_exceeded`).
QoS 1 and QoS 2 publishes are rejected with a `quota exceeded` reason code rather than
dropped, and were only visible in the packet-level `packets.publish.quota_exceeded`
counter.

QoS 1 and QoS 2 rejections are now counted in `messages.rejected.quota_exceeded`
(Prometheus: `emqx_messages_rejected_quota_exceeded`), so the rate limiter's effect is
observable per QoS level: dropped for QoS 0, rejected for QoS 1 and QoS 2. The total
number of publishes stopped by the rate limiter across all QoS levels remains available
as `packets.publish.quota_exceeded`.
