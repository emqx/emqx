Previously, Sources/Actions would be stopped asynchronously when restarting the `emqx_bridge` application, and Connectors would not be removed/stopped when stopping the `emqx_connector` application, which could manifest as connections still made to remote systems even after a node joins a cluster without that Connector, or potentially some resource leaks due to Actions/Sources not being removed properly.  Now, Sources/Actions are stopped synchronously and Connectors removed.
