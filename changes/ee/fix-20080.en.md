Refused new client connections when the node is close to its Erlang process limit, so that the node itself stays up under a high rate of new connections.

When the number of Erlang processes reaches 90% of the process limit, MQTT and gateway listeners close each new connection at once. A TCP, SSL or QUIC connection is closed before the TLS handshake starts. A WebSocket connection gets HTTP 503. The remaining processes stay available to the processes that keep the node and the cluster running. The listener shutdown counters show the refusals as `too_many_processes`, and the log shows a throttled `connection_refused_due_to_process_limit` warning.
