Added `DELETE /api/v5/queue/:name/messages`, which deletes the messages of a message queue and keeps the queue, its settings and its consumer progress.

The optional `before` parameter limits the deletion to messages stored before that time. It takes an RFC 3339 timestamp or milliseconds since the epoch. Deleted messages are not delivered again, including ones a subscriber received but had not acknowledged. Delivery to the queue's subscribers pauses for about twice the queue's ping interval. Disk space is freed later, when the storage compacts.
