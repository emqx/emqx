A TLS alert that closes an established connection no longer produces an error-level supervisor report. Each alert now increases a listener shutdown counter named by the alert kind, for example `user_canceled` or `bad_record_mac`, and is still logged, at notice level. Alert codes that the TLS stack does not recognize share one counter, `unsupported/unknown_alert`. Other socket errors that previously produced the same error report, such as an invalid proxy protocol header, are counted as `sock_error` and logged at notice level. The counters are shown by `emqx ctl listeners` and in the `emqx_client_disconnected_reason` Prometheus metric.
