With `parse_unit = frame` (the default), TCP and SSL listeners now apply `mqtt.max_connect_packet_size` at the transport: a first packet larger than the limit is refused from its fixed header, before its body is buffered. Previously the transport buffered up to `mqtt.max_packet_size` before the check ran. The limit goes back to `mqtt.max_packet_size` once the client is connected. Defaults are unchanged (both limits are `1MB`), so nothing changes until `mqtt.max_connect_packet_size` is lowered.
