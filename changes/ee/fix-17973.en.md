Fixed Kafka producer action retry metrics. The `retried`, `retried.success`, and `retried.failed` counters on an action's metrics now reflect messages that the internal buffer re-sends after a broker reconnect, so an operator can tell whether retried messages ultimately succeeded or failed. Previously these counters stayed at `0` regardless of how many internal retries occurred. The `success` and `failed` counters are unaffected and are not double-counted.
