Durable session fixes:

- Fix a problem where durable storage failed to release RocksDB column family handles after dropping slabs,
  preventing deallocation of the disk space until the next node restart.

- Add retry mechanism for the durable timers.
  This feature is used to make expiration of durable sessions more reliable.

- Safety improvements for the durable session expiration mechanism:
  expiry timer is now guarded by the channel cookie.
  It will never delete data of durable sessions that have been taken over,
  even if a net-split prevents cancellation of the old timer during the take over.

- Fix reporting of `awaiting_rel_cnt` counter for disconnected durable sessions.
