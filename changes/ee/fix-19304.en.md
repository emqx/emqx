Important fixes related to durable sessions:

- Fixed race condition during takeover of durable sessions that allowed the new connection to read stale session data.

- Durable session tear-down procedure has been made one-way only,
  ruling out possible issues arising from restoration and the continued use of half-closed sessions.

- Fixed a problem where durable storage failed to release RocksDB column family handles after dropping slabs,
  preventing deallocation of the disk space until the next node restart.

- Added retry mechanism for the durable timers.
  This feature is used to make expiration of durable sessions more reliable.

- Safety improvements for the durable session expiration mechanism:
  expiry timer is now guarded by the channel cookie.
  It will never delete data of durable sessions that have been taken over,
  even if a net-split prevents cancellation of the old timer during the take over.

- Fixed reporting of `awaiting_rel_cnt` counter for disconnected durable sessions.
