Enabled TCP keepalive on connections of HTTP-based connectors, such as HTTP Server, GCP PubSub, BigQuery, Couchbase and Snowflake, and of HTTP authentication and authorization.

These connections are opened with a 30-second keepalive, which used to have no effect. On Linux and macOS, an idle connection now sends a keepalive probe after 30 seconds and then every 5 seconds, and is closed after 3 unanswered probes. On other systems, keepalive is enabled with the OS timers.
