Fixed rare cases where a client that consumes a stream or retained messages stopped receiving messages, or was disconnected because its connection process crashed. This could happen after a session takeover, after the session dropped messages (for example because of No Local or message expiry), or when the delivery rate limit was reached.

When the delivery rate limit is reached, messages from streams and message queues now wait at their source instead of in the session message queue. QoS 0 messages that the delivery rate limit drops now count in the `delivery.dropped` metric and trigger the `delivery.dropped` event.
