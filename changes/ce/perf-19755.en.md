Enabled periodic garbage collection by default, with an interval of 30 minutes, and changed it to work incrementally.

It now walks the connected clients of the node and collects a few of them at a time, spread over `node.global_gc_interval`, instead of collecting every process on the node at once. This removes the periodic spike in CPU load that the previous collection caused. A client that is hibernated has already released its memory and is skipped. Set `node.global_gc_interval` to `disabled` to turn it off.
